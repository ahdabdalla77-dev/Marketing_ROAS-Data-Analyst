Digital Marketing Paid Ads Performance Report
1. Core Performance & Baseline Metrics (Overall KPIs)
Return on Ad Spend (ROAS): Standing at a strong 4.01. This means for every dollar invested across all channels, the business generates roughly $4 in revenue, indicating a highly profitable baseline.

Click-Through Rate (CTR): A healthy 4.23%, proving that the ad creatives, messaging, and audience targeting are compelling and highly engaging.

Conversion Rate (CR): At 1.17%. While this is acceptable for e-commerce, it leaves room for optimization given how high the initial click-through interest (CTR) is.

Channel Breakdown (Revenue vs. Spend):
Google Ads: The undisputed champion, bringing in 584.23K in revenue on a minimal ad spend of just 30.12K.

Instagram: The second best performer, generating 237.76K in revenue with an ad spend of 46.8K.

Facebook: Generated 162.64K in revenue but demanded the highest budget allocation at 113.2K.

TikTok: The lowest performer commercially, yielding only 24.8K in revenue.

2. Key Insights & Critical Red Flags
The Facebook Budget Bleed (ROAS = 1): Facebook is swallowing the largest portion of the budget (~113.2K) while delivering a ROAS of exactly 1.00. This means the platform is barely breaking even on direct ad spend, draining financial resources without contributing to net profit.

The TikTok Paradox (High Awareness, Zero Sales): TikTok smashed performance in top-of-funnel metrics, scoring the highest visibility across the board with 1.8M Impressions and 1.4M Reach. However, its ROAS is 0.00. It is highly effective for brand exposure but fails entirely at driving direct conversions.

Inverted Budget Allocation: The account is currently suffering from poor budget distribution. Our most profitable channel (Google Ads) receives the lowest funding, while our least efficient channels (Facebook & TikTok) absorb the most spend.

3. Strategic Forecasts & Recommendations
Aggressive Budget Reallocation:

Recommendation: Immediately shift 40% to 50% of the budget away from Facebook and TikTok and inject it directly into Google Ads. Google boasts an extraordinary ROAS of 19.00. Scaling this budget will maximize top-line revenue rapidly.

Pivot TikTok's Campaign Objective:

Forecast: Expecting TikTok to drive immediate e-commerce purchases with the current setup is unrealistic.

Recommendation: Stop conversion-focused campaigns on TikTok. Instead, treat it strictly as a Brand Awareness tool or completely change the product presentation style to appeal better to Gen-Z shopping behavior.

Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) on the Website:

Insight: The gap between a high CTR (4.23%) and a modest CR (1.17%) means users love the ads and click on them, but drop off once they reach the website.

Recommendation: Audit the landing page and checkout user experience (UX). Review page load speeds, mobile responsiveness, payment options, and shipping barriers to fix the checkout drop-offs.

Executive Summary for Management:
"Our digital marketing engine is highly efficient, yielding an overall 4.01 ROAS, driven by outstanding creative engagement (4.23% CTR). However, profitability is capped due to skewed budget distribution. Google Ads is our primary growth driver with an unmatched 19.00 ROAS, while Facebook is breaking even (1.00 ROAS), and TikTok functions purely as an awareness vehicle (0.00 ROAS despite 1.8M impressions). HR and Marketing recommend scaling back Facebook spend, doubling down on Google Ads, and initiating a website CRO audit to maximize our conversion rates in the upcoming quarter."
